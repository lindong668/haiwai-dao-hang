Contact Us

Updated Oct 2026

Questions, corrections, tool suggestions and review requests are all welcome. This page explains where to send them and what happens next.

On this pageEmail What To Send What We Cannot Help With Advertising

Email

The fastest way to reach us is email:

yokok8@outlook.com

We read every message. Substantive ones get a reply, usually within a few days.

What To Send

Corrections

The most valuable message you can send. Free plan limits, file size caps and pricing on these tools change often, and a review that was accurate six months ago may now be wrong. Tell us which tool and what changed, and we will verify and update it.

Tool suggestions

If you use a free tool that deserves a place here, send the URL and a line about what you use it for. We test anything that looks like a genuine fit.

Review requests

If a tool you need sits in a category we have not covered yet, tell us what you are trying to do. Gaps in the directory get filled based on what people actually ask for.

Removing a link

If you own a tool listed here and would rather it not be included, say so and we will remove it.

What We Cannot Help With

To set expectations, we cannot provide:

  • Technical support for any third-party tool — contact that tool's own support instead
  • Help with paid plans, billing or refunds for other services
  • Guaranteed response times, since this is a small independent site

Advertising

For advertising or partnership enquiries, use the same address with a clear subject line. Note that we do not sell placements inside reviews or accept payment to change what a review says.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your email address?

yokok8@outlook.com — it is the only official contact address for this site.

How quickly do you reply?

Substantive messages usually get a reply within a few days, though there is no guaranteed response time.

Can you fix a tool that is not working?

No — we have no control over third-party tools. Contact that service's own support. We can, however, correct our review if it is inaccurate.

Can I suggest a tool to add?

Yes, please do. Send the URL and a sentence about how you use it.

Can I ask for my tool to be removed?

Yes. Tell us and it will be taken down.
