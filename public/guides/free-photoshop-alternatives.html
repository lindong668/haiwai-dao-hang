What You Give Up And What You Do Not你放弃什么，又没放弃什么

Being honest about the trade-off saves disappointment. Browser editors do not replace Photoshop for:坦白讲清楚取舍，能省掉失望。浏览器编辑器在以下方面无法替代 Photoshop：

  • Complex multi-layer compositing over long sessions需要长时间进行的复杂多层合成
  • Advanced 3D, video timelines or RAW processing pipelines高级 3D、视频时间轴或 RAW 处理流程
  • CMYK print production with colour management带色彩管理的 CMYK 印刷输出
  • Very large files measured in hundreds of megabytes几百 MB 级别的超大文件

They do handle, comfortably:而下面这些，它们能轻松应付：

  • Layered editing, masks and adjustment layers图层编辑、蒙版和调整图层
  • Retouching, cropping, resizing and colour correction修图、裁剪、改尺寸和色彩校正
  • Background removal and compositing去背景与合成
  • Export to web formats, including PSD in some cases导出为网页格式，有些还支持 PSD

For a large majority of everyday image work, the free option is genuinely sufficient.对绝大多数日常图片工作来说，免费方案是真的够用。

PhotopeaPhotopea

Photopea is the closest thing to Photoshop in a browser tab. The layout, the shortcuts and the tool behaviour are deliberately familiar: layers panel on the right, toolbar on the left, and Ctrl+J, Ctrl+T and the rest working as your hands expect.Photopea 是浏览器标签页里最接近 Photoshop 的东西。布局、快捷键和工具行为都是刻意做得让人熟悉：图层面板在右，工具栏在左，Ctrl+J、Ctrl+T 这些也按你手上的习惯来。

Its standout capability is format support. It opens PSD, XD, Sketch, Figma, AI, EPS, PDF and RAW files, so a design file received from a colleague can be opened on a machine with no design software at all. Files process locally, and there is no watermark.它最突出的是格式支持。它能打开 PSD、XD、Sketch、Figma、AI、EPS、PDF 和 RAW 文件，所以同事发来的设计稿，在一台完全没装设计软件的机器上也能打开。文件在本地处理，也没有水印。

The compromises are interface ads around the edges and weaker performance on very large files.代价是界面边缘有广告，以及处理超大文件时性能偏弱。

PiccalcPicCalc

PicCalc takes a different approach: rather than one deep editor, it offers 24 focused tools — background removal, ID photo creation, resizing, conversion, compression, filters, watermarking, EXIF removal, QR generation and more.PicCalc 走的是另一条路：它不是做一个大而全的编辑器，而是提供 24 个针对性工具 —— 去背景、证件照制作、改尺寸、格式转换、压缩、滤镜、加水印、清除 EXIF、生成二维码等等。

Everything is processed locally in the browser, so images never leave your device. For anyone working with ID documents, contracts, medical images or client artwork, that is a decisive advantage over cloud editors. There is no quota and no watermark.全部在浏览器本地处理，图片不会离开你的设备。对需要处理证件、合同、医学影像或客户稿件的人来说，这比云端编辑器有决定性优势。没有次数限制，也没有水印。

It is not a layered compositing tool, but for the "I need to do one specific thing to this image" case it is usually faster than opening a full editor.它不是图层合成工具，但对于「我只想对这张图做一件特定的事」这种场景，它通常比打开一个完整编辑器更快。

CanvaCanva

Canva is a design platform rather than a photo editor, and that distinction matters. If you are producing a social graphic, presentation slide, poster or resume, its templates and asset library save enormous time.Canva 是一个设计平台，而不是图片编辑器，这个区别很重要。如果你要做社交配图、演示幻灯片、海报或简历，它的模板和素材库能省下大量时间。

If you are editing a photograph — retouching, colour correcting, cutting out a subject — it is the wrong tool. Use a dedicated editor, then import the finished image into Canva for layout.但如果你是在修一张照片 —— 修瑕疵、校色、抠主体 —— 那它就选错了。应该先用专业编辑器处理，再把成品导入 Canva 排版。

GimpGIMP

GIMP deserves a mention as the option for people who want a real desktop application with no subscription. It is genuinely powerful, fully offline and free.GIMP 值得提一句，它适合那些想要一个真正桌面应用、又不想付订阅费的人。它确实强大，完全离线，而且免费。

Its cost is the interface, which does not follow Photoshop conventions and requires real adjustment. Choose it if you need offline heavy editing and are willing to learn it; choose a browser tool if you want results today.它的代价是界面 —— 不遵循 Photoshop 的习惯，需要实实在在地适应。如果你需要离线做重度编辑、并且愿意学，就选它；想今天就出结果，就选浏览器工具。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

What is the best free alternative to Photoshop?最好的免费 Photoshop 替代品是什么？

For layered editing in a browser, Photopea — it closely mirrors Photoshop's interface, shortcuts and PSD support. For quick single-purpose jobs and privacy, PicCalc is faster.浏览器里的图层编辑选 Photopea —— 它高度复刻了 Photoshop 的界面、快捷键和 PSD 支持。单纯做某件事、或看重隐私，PicCalc 更快。

Can I open PSD files without Photoshop?没有 Photoshop 能打开 PSD 吗？

Yes. Photopea opens PSD with layers, masks and text intact, and can re-export as PSD.可以。Photopea 打开 PSD 会保留图层、蒙版和文字，还能重新导出为 PSD。

Is there a free editor that does not upload my photos?有不上传照片的免费编辑器吗？

Yes. Both Photopea and PicCalc process images locally in the browser, so files never leave your device.有。Photopea 和 PicCalc 都在浏览器本地处理图片，文件不会离开你的设备。

Do I need to install anything?需要安装什么吗？

No. Photopea, PicCalc and Canva all run in a browser tab. GIMP requires installation but works fully offline.不需要。Photopea、PicCalc 和 Canva 都在浏览器标签页里运行。GIMP 需要安装，但完全离线可用。

Can these handle professional print work?它们能胜任专业印刷吗？

Not reliably. CMYK colour management and large-format output are where desktop Photoshop still matters.不太行。CMYK 色彩管理和大幅面输出，仍然是桌面版 Photoshop 的领域。

Are free editors watermarked?免费编辑器会给图片打水印吗？

Photopea and PicCalc do not watermark output. Some tools place features behind a paid plan rather than marking your file.Photopea 和 PicCalc 都不会加水印。有些工具是把功能放进付费方案，而不是在你的文件上打标记。
