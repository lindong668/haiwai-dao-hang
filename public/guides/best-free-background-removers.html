Removing a background used to mean tracing around a subject with a pen tool for twenty minutes. Now several free tools do it automatically in seconds — but they differ enormously on the things that actually matter: how good the free download is, whether your photo gets uploaded, and whether the edges survive close inspection.过去去个背景，意味着要用钢笔工具沿着主体描二十分钟。现在好几个免费工具几秒钟就能自动搞定——但它们在真正要紧的事情上差别巨大：免费下载的质量如何、你的照片会不会被上传、以及边缘经不经得起细看。

How We Compared

We judged each tool on five criteria that affect real users rather than marketing claims:我们按五项真正影响用户的标准来评判，而不是看宣传说法：

  • Edge quality — how cleanly it handles hair, fur, glass and fine detail边缘质量——处理头发、毛发、玻璃和细小细节的干净程度
  • Free output — the actual resolution you get without paying, not just whether a button exists免费输出——不花钱实际能拿到的分辨率，而不只是「有没有这个按钮」
  • Privacy — whether your image is uploaded to a server or processed locally隐私——图片是上传到服务器，还是在本地处理
  • Ease of use — how many steps between arriving and downloading易用性——从进入到下载之间要走几步
  • Limits — quotas, watermarks and daily caps限制——次数、水印和每日上限
The headline findingThe best tool depends almost entirely on two questions: do you need full resolution, and is the image confidential? Those two answers eliminate most of the field immediately.

Remove Bg

Remove.bg remains the benchmark for automatic quality. Its model is trained specifically on foreground separation, and it handles flyaway hair, pet fur and bicycle spokes better than generic selection tools. Processing takes about five seconds and there is no learning curve at all.Remove.bg 仍是自动抠图质量的标杆。它的模型专门针对前景分离训练，处理飞散的头发、宠物毛发和自行车辐条都优于通用选择工具。处理约五秒完成，而且完全没有学习成本。

The catch is resolution. Without paying, you download a preview roughly 500–600 pixels on the long edge. That is perfectly usable for a slide, a mockup or a quick test, and completely unusable for print or for a product listing where customers zoom in. Full resolution requires credits.问题在分辨率。不付费的话，你下载到的是长边约 500–600 像素的预览图。做幻灯片、样机图或快速测试完全够用，但用于印刷或顾客会放大的商品详情页则完全不行。全分辨率需要积分。

It also uploads your image, which rules it out for ID scans, contracts and anything else you would not want sitting on someone else's server.它还会上传你的图片，这就不适合处理证件扫描件、合同，以及任何你不想让它留在别人服务器上的东西。

Piccalc

PicCalc takes the opposite trade-off. Its edge quality is good rather than class-leading — clear subjects separate cleanly, though very fine hair may want a touch-up — but two things make it the right answer for a lot of people.PicCalc 做了相反的取舍。它的边缘质量算不错，但不是顶尖——主体清晰时能干净分离，不过极细的发丝可能仍需修补——但有两点让它成为很多人的正确答案。

First, nothing is uploaded. All processing happens in your browser, so the file never leaves your device. You can verify this in the network tab, and most tools keep working even after you disconnect from the internet. Second, the free output is full resolution with no watermark and no quota.

It also bundles the related steps. After removing a background you can switch to the ID Photo tool to place the subject on a red, blue or white backdrop, then compress — all in the same tab, all locally. For anyone preparing official ID or visa photos, that combination is genuinely hard to find elsewhere for free.它还把相关步骤打包在一起。抠完背景后，你可以切到证件照工具，把主体放到红底、蓝底或白底上，然后再压缩——全在同一个标签页里、全在本地完成。对任何要准备官方证件照或签证照的人来说，这种组合在其他地方确实很难免费找到。

Photopea

Photopea is the choice when automatic is not good enough. It will not remove a background with one click, but it gives you layers, masks, the pen tool and refinement brushes — so when the AI misses a strand of hair or a semi-transparent veil, you can fix it precisely.当自动抠图不够好时，Photopea 就是答案。它不能一键去背景，但它给你图层、蒙版、钢笔工具和细化画笔——所以当 AI 漏掉一缕头发或一层半透明薄纱时，你可以精确修好。

It processes locally like PicCalc, exports at full resolution, and costs nothing. The cost is your time: expect several minutes rather than several seconds, plus a learning curve if you have never used a layered editor.它像 PicCalc 一样在本地处理，全分辨率导出，而且完全免费。代价是你的时间：预计要几分钟而不是几秒钟，如果你从没用过图层编辑器，还有一段学习曲线。

A sensible workflow is to run an automatic tool first and use Photopea only to clean up the difficult areas.明智的流程是：先用自动工具跑一遍，然后只用 Photopea 来处理困难的区域。

Canva And Adobe Express

Canva and Adobe Express both include background removal, and both make sense only in one situation: you are already designing in that platform. If your graphic is already on a Canva canvas, using its built-in remover avoids an export-and-reimport round trip.Canva 和 Adobe Express 都带抠图功能，但它们只在一种情况下合理：你本来就在那个平台里做设计。如果你的图稿已经在 Canva 画布上，用它内置的抠图能省掉导出再导入的往返。

As standalone background removers they are weaker choices. Canva puts the feature behind a Pro subscription, and both upload your images. Neither is the tool to pick when background removal is the actual task rather than one step inside a larger design.作为独立的抠图工具，它们都是较弱的选择。Canva 把这个功能放在 Pro 订阅后面，而且两者都会上传你的图片。当抠图本身就是任务、而不是某个大设计中的一步时，这两个都不是该选的工具。

Getting Better Results

Whichever tool you pick, the source photograph determines most of the outcome. A few rules that reliably improve results:无论你选哪个工具，原始照片都决定了大部分结果。以下几条规则能稳定提升效果：

  • Shoot against contrast. A dark-haired subject against a light wall separates far more cleanly than the same subject against a dark background.拍摄时制造反差。深发色主体配浅色墙，比同一个主体配深色背景分离得干净得多。
  • Avoid busy backgrounds. Foliage, crowds and patterned wallpaper are where automatic segmentation struggles most.避开杂乱背景。枝叶、人群和花纹壁纸是自动分割最容易翻车的地方。
  • Light the subject evenly. Harsh shadows read as part of the subject and get retained.给主体均匀打光。浓重的阴影会被当成主体的一部分而保留下来。
  • Start with the largest source you have. Downscaling after cut-out preserves edge quality better than cutting out an already small image.从你手上最大的原图开始。抠完再缩小，比直接抠一张本来就小的图更能保住边缘质量。
  • Inspect at 100% zoom. Problems invisible in a thumbnail are obvious at full size.在 100% 缩放下检查。缩略图里看不出的问题，在原始尺寸下一目了然。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

What is the best free background remover?最好的免费抠图工具是哪个？

Remove.bg produces the best automatic results, but its free download is only about 500-600px. PicCalc gives full resolution for free with no upload, which makes it the better choice if you need a usable file or the image is confidential.Remove.bg 的自动效果最好，但免费下载只有约 500-600px。PicCalc 免费给全分辨率且不上传，所以如果你需要能直接用的文件、或图片涉密，它是更好的选择。

Can I remove a background without uploading my photo?不上传照片也能去背景吗？

Yes. Both PicCalc and Photopea process entirely in your browser, so the image never leaves your device.有。PicCalc 和 Photopea 都完全在你的浏览器里处理，图片不会离开你的设备。

Why is my free download so small?为什么免费下载的图这么小？

Many services treat the free tier as a preview: you can see the result but only download a reduced resolution. Full size requires credits or a subscription.很多服务把免费版当成预览：你能看到结果，但只能下载降分辨率版本。原尺寸需要积分或订阅。

How do I remove a background from hair cleanly?头发部分怎么抠得干净？

Start with a high-contrast source photograph, use a tool trained on hair like Remove.bg, and refine any remaining strands manually in a layered editor such as Photopea.先用高反差的原始照片，用像 Remove.bg 这样针对头发训练过的工具，再在 Photopea 这类图层编辑器里手动修补残留的发丝。

Is there a completely free option with no limits?有完全免费、无限制的选择吗？

PicCalc offers full-resolution output with no watermark and no quota, processing locally in the browser. Photopea is also free and unlimited but requires manual work.PicCalc 提供全分辨率输出，无水印、无次数限制，且在浏览器本地处理。Photopea 同样免费且无限制，但需要手动操作。

Can I put a white or blue background behind my ID photo?能给证件照换成白底或蓝底吗？

Yes. PicCalc's ID Photo tool swaps the background colour, including the white, red and blue backdrops required by different official specifications.可以。PicCalc 的证件照工具能更换背景色，包括不同官方规范所要求的白底、红底和蓝底。
