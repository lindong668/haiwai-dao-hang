How to Compress Images Without Losing Quality: A Practical Guide如何压缩图片又不损失画质：一份实用指南

Updated Oct 20262026年10月更新7 min read分钟阅读Comparison guide对比指南

A single unedited photo from a modern phone can be 5MB. Put ten of those on a page and you have built something most visitors will abandon before it finishes loading. The good news is that most of that weight is unnecessary, and removing it costs almost nothing in visible quality.现代手机拍的一张未处理照片可能就有 5MB。把十张这样的图放进一个页面，你做出来的东西大多数访客在加载完之前就会放弃。好消息是：这些体积里绝大部分都是多余的，而去掉它们几乎不会损失可见画质。

Why Images Are So Large

An image file stores a value for every pixel. A 12-megapixel photo contains twelve million of them, and before compression each one needs three colour channels. That is roughly 36MB of raw data before any encoding happens.图像文件为每一个像素存储一个数值。一张 1200 万像素的照片包含一千两百万个像素，而在压缩之前每个像素都需要三个颜色通道。也就是说，在做任何编码之前，原始数据约有 36MB。

Compression is what brings that down to something manageable, and it works in two fundamentally different ways — which is where most of the confusion comes from.压缩正是把这些体积降到可控范围的东西，而它的工作方式有两种根本不同的类型——多数困惑也正来源于此。

Lossy Vs Lossless

Type类型What it does功能说明Typical saving典型压缩率Use for适用场景
LosslessRewrites data more efficiently; every pixel is recoverable exactly更高效地重写数据；每个像素都能精确还原5-20%Logos, screenshots with text, source fileslogo、带文字的截图、源文件
LossyDiscards detail the eye is unlikely to notice丢弃眼睛不太可能察觉的细节50-80%Photographs, web images

The word "lossy" sounds alarming, but applied sensibly the discarded information is genuinely imperceptible. A well-compressed JPEG at quality 80 is very hard to distinguish from the original at normal viewing size, while being a fraction of the size.「有损」这个词听起来吓人，但用得合理的话，被丢弃的信息确实察觉不到。一张质量 80、压缩得当的 JPEG，在正常观看尺寸下很难与原图区分，而体积却只有原图的零头。

Lossless matters where precision is required. A screenshot containing text compressed with JPEG produces muddy, fringing characters; PNG keeps them crisp. That is why the rule of thumb exists at all.在需要精确度的地方，无损才重要。一张含文字的截图用 JPEG 压缩后，字符会糊掉、出现毛边；PNG 则能让它们保持清晰。这条经验法则正是因此才存在。

If you are publishing to the web and not constrained by legacy support, WebP is usually the right answer. It supports both lossy and lossless modes plus transparency, and it is supported by every current browser.

Step By Step

  1. Resize first. This is the biggest win and the one most people skip. If your page displays an image at 800px wide, there is no reason to serve a 4000px file. Resizing before compressing typically accounts for most of the saving.先改尺寸。这是收益最大的一步，也是最多人跳过的一步。如果你的页面把图片显示为 800px 宽，那就没有理由去提供一张 4000px 的图。压缩前先改尺寸，通常就占了大头。
  2. Pick the format. Photographs to JPEG or WebP; anything with text, flat colour or transparency to PNG or lossless WebP.选择格式。照片用 JPEG 或 WebP；带文字、纯色块或透明通道的用 PNG 或无损 WebP。
  3. Compress with a quality slider. Start around 80 and reduce until you see degradation, then step back up one notch.用质量滑块压缩。从 80 左右开始往下调，直到看见画质劣化，然后再往回调一档。
  4. Inspect at 100% zoom. Check faces, skies and gradients — these show banding and artefacts first.在 100% 缩放下检查。重点看人脸、天空和渐变——这些地方最先出现色带和压缩伪影。
  5. Compare the numbers. Note the saving percentage so you can repeat the same settings next time.对比数字。记下压缩比例，这样下次就能复用同样的设置。

Tools That Do It Well

TinyPNG / TinyJPG

The default choice for most developers. It reduces the colour palette rather than blurring pixels, typically saving 50–70% with no visible difference on screenshots and flat graphics. Free with a batch limit, and it preserves PNG transparency — which naive JPEG conversion destroys.多数开发者的默认选择。它靠减少调色板颜色而不是糊掉像素来压缩，通常能省 50–70%，而截图和纯色图形肉眼看不出差别。免费版有单次批量限制，并且会保留 PNG 透明通道——而简单转 JPEG 会把它破坏掉。

PicCalc

Worth choosing when images are confidential, because compression happens locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded. It also shows the size before and after, so you can see exactly what you saved.当图片涉密时值得选它，因为压缩在你的浏览器本地完成，什么都不上传。它还会显示压缩前后的体积，让你清楚看到到底省了多少。

Convertio or CloudConvert

Better when you also need a format change — for example converting a batch of PNGs to WebP, which is often a larger saving than compressing within the same format.当你同时需要换格式时更好——比如把一批 PNG 转成 WebP，这往往比在同一种格式内压缩省得更多。

tip最大的一招 先改尺寸再压缩，比死磕压缩参数有效得多。一张 4000px 的照片缩到 1200px 再轻度压缩，会比原分辨率下重度压缩小很多。

Common Mistakes

  • Compressing without resizing. The most common error, and the one that leaves the most size on the table.只压缩不改尺寸。这是最常见的错误，也是最能省却没省下来的那一项。
  • Using JPEG for screenshots. Text and UI edges acquire visible artefacts; use PNG or lossless WebP.给截图用 JPEG。文字和 UI 边缘会出现明显伪影；应该用 PNG 或无损 WebP。
  • Compressing an already-compressed file repeatedly. Each generation degrades quality further with diminishing size returns. Always work from the original.反复压缩一个已经被压缩过的文件。每多压一代，画质进一步劣化，而体积收益递减。永远从原图开始处理。
  • Ignoring transparency. Converting PNG to JPEG silently drops the alpha channel and usually fills it with white.忽略透明通道。把 PNG 转成 JPEG 会悄悄丢掉 alpha 通道，并通常用白色填充。
  • Embedding large images as Base64. This inflates size by about 33% and prevents separate caching.把大图以 Base64 嵌入。这会让体积膨胀约 33%，还会导致无法单独缓存。
  • Never checking the result. Automated settings occasionally ruin an image; a glance at 100% zoom catches it.从不检查结果。自动设置偶尔会毁掉一张图；在 100% 缩放下瞥一眼就能发现。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

How much can I compress without visible quality loss?压到什么程度还看不出画质损失？

Typically 50-70% for photographs using lossy compression, and noticeably more if you also resize to the dimensions you actually display.照片用有损压缩通常能省 50-70%，如果你还把尺寸改成实际显示的尺寸，省得会明显更多。

Is lossy compression bad?有损压缩不好吗？

Not at sensible settings. It discards detail the eye generally cannot perceive. It is only a problem if you compress repeatedly or push quality very low.在合理设置下不会。它丢弃的是眼睛通常察觉不到的细节。只有当你反复压缩，或把质量压得非常低时才会出问题。

Should I use WebP instead of JPEG or PNG?该用 WebP 替代 JPEG 或 PNG 吗？

For web publishing, usually yes. WebP is typically 25-35% smaller at equivalent quality and supports transparency, with support in all current browsers.用于网络发布的话，通常是的。WebP 在同等画质下通常小 25-35%，且支持透明度，所有现代浏览器都支持。

Why does my screenshot look bad after compressing?为什么我的截图压缩后就难看了？

You probably used JPEG. Screenshots with text and flat UI need PNG or lossless WebP to stay crisp.你大概用了 JPEG。带文字和扁平 UI 的截图需要 PNG 或无损 WebP 才能保持清晰。

Does resizing really matter that much?改尺寸真的有那么重要吗？

Yes — it is usually the single largest saving. Serving an image at the size it is displayed beats any compression setting.是的——这通常是最省的一项。按实际显示尺寸提供图片，胜过任何压缩设置。

Can I compress images without uploading them?不上传图片也能压缩吗？

Yes. PicCalc performs compression locally in your browser, so the file never leaves your device.可以。PicCalc 在你的浏览器本地完成压缩，文件不会离开你的设备。
