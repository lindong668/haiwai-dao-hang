What We Do我们做什么

There is no shortage of pages listing "100 best free tools". Most are assembled from other people's lists without anyone opening the tools. That produces entries describing features that no longer exist and free plans that were changed years ago.网上的「100 款最佳免费工具」列表一点也不少。大多数是把别人的清单拼一拼，压根没人真的去打开那些工具。结果就是条目里描述的早已不存在的功能，以及几年前就已经改过的免费方案。

We take a narrower approach. This directory covers a smaller number of tools, each of which was opened, run on a real file, and written up from that experience. When a review says the free download is roughly 500 pixels, that is because we downloaded one and measured it.我们的做法更窄。本站收录的工具数量更少，但每一款都是打开过、在真实文件上跑过，并且照着那次体验写出来的。当测评里说免费下载大约只有 500 像素，那是因为我们真的下载了一张并量过。

Our Review Criteria我们的测评标准

Every review answers the same six questions:每篇测评都回答同样的六个问题：

  1. What does it actually do? Described in plain language, not the marketing summary.它到底是干什么的？ 用大白话讲，而不是照抄宣传文案。
  2. How do you use it? Numbered steps from arriving to downloading.怎么用？ 从进入页面到下载结果的分步说明。
  3. What does free really include? The part most listings skip — resolution caps, daily task counts, file size limits and watermarks.「免费」到底包含什么？ 大多数清单都跳过这一节 —— 分辨率上限、每日任务次数、文件大小限制和水印。
  4. What works well? The genuine strengths.哪些地方好用？ 真正的优势。
  5. Where does it fall short? Stated plainly. A tool that is wrong for your job should say so.哪些地方不行？ 直说。如果你的活不适合用它，就该明讲。
  6. Who is it for? A concrete recommendation rather than "everyone".适合谁用？ 给出具体建议，而不是一句「所有人都适合」。

Free-plan details are the section we spend most time on, because "free" is used loosely across this category. A service can advertise itself as free while only letting you download a preview too small to use.免费方案细节是我们花时间最多的一节，因为在这个品类里「免费」这个词用得很随意。一个服务可以自称免费，却只让你下载一张小到根本没法用的预览图。

What We Will Not Do我们不做的事

  • We do not list a tool we have not used.我们自己没用过的工具，不收录。
  • We do not present a tool's own marketing copy as our assessment.不把工具自己的宣传文案当成我们的评价。
  • We do not rank tools by commission, because we take none.不按佣金高低排序，因为我们不收任何佣金。
  • We do not claim a free plan is more generous than it is.不把免费方案说得比实际更慷慨。
