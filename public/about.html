About This Site

What We Do

There is no shortage of pages listing "100 best free tools". Most are assembled from other people's lists without anyone opening the tools. That produces entries describing features that no longer exist and free plans that were changed years ago.

We take a narrower approach. This directory covers a smaller number of tools, each of which was opened, run on a real file, and written up from that experience. When a review says the free download is roughly 500 pixels, that is because we downloaded one and measured it.

Our Review Criteria

Every review answers the same six questions:

  1. What does it actually do? Described in plain language, not the marketing summary.
  2. How do you use it? Numbered steps from arriving to downloading.
  3. What does free really include? The part most listings skip — resolution caps, daily task counts, file size limits and watermarks.
  4. What works well? The genuine strengths.
  5. Where does it fall short? Stated plainly. A tool that is wrong for your job should say so.
  6. Who is it for? A concrete recommendation rather than "everyone".

Free-plan details are the section we spend most time on, because "free" is used loosely across this category. A service can advertise itself as free while only letting you download a preview too small to use.

What We Will Not Do

  • We do not list a tool we have not used.
  • We do not present a tool's own marketing copy as our assessment.
  • We do not rank tools by commission, because we take none.
  • We do not claim a free plan is more generous than it is.

Our Own Tools

Two of the tools reviewed here are ours, and we say so every time they appear:

  • PicCalc — 24 image tools that run entirely inside your browser, so images are never uploaded. Free, full resolution, no watermark.
  • CalcHub — 9 finance calculators (mortgage, APR, payoff, loan comparison, affordability, prepayment and currency) that show the formula behind every result. Free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

They are listed because they are genuinely useful, and they are marked as ours so you can weigh our assessment accordingly.

Affiliate And Advertising

We currently take no affiliate commission from any tool listed. The site displays advertising, including Google AdSense, which is how the running cost is covered. Advertising does not influence which tools are listed or what the reviews say.

Suggest A Tool

If you know a tool that belongs here, or you have found that something in a review is now wrong, email yokok8@outlook.com. Corrections are especially welcome — free plan limits change often, and we would rather fix a review than leave it misleading.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is every tool really tested?

Yes. Each listed tool was opened and used on a real file before being written up. We would rather list fewer tools than describe ones we have not run.

Do you get paid for listings?

No. We take no affiliate commission from any tool listed here. Advertising on the site does not affect what we recommend.

Why do you list your own tools?

Because they are genuinely useful and we can describe them accurately from first-hand knowledge. They are always clearly marked as ours.

How often are reviews updated?

Free plan limits and pricing change frequently, so reviews note that readers should confirm current terms. If you spot something out of date, email us and we will correct it.

Can I suggest a tool?

Yes — email yokok8@outlook.com. We test anything that looks like a genuine fit.
