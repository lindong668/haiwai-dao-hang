What It Is这是什么

The interface is the main draw: every tool is a single drop zone with no configuration, which makes it fast for one-off jobs. Upload a PDF, pick what you want, download the result.它最大的吸引力是界面：每个工具就是一个拖拽区，无需任何配置，处理一次性任务非常快。上传 PDF、选好要做什么、下载结果。

The free plan is genuinely usable but constrained. There is a daily task allowance and file-size limits, so it suits occasional work rather than a regular workflow. Heavy or daily use means a subscription.免费版确实能用，但受限。有每日任务额度和文件大小限制，所以适合偶尔用用，不适合当作常规工作流。高频或每天使用就得订阅。

Tool coverage includes conversion both ways (PDF to Word, Excel, PPT, JPG and back), compression, merging, splitting, page rotation, e-signature, unlocking password-protected files, and OCR on paid plans.工具覆盖双向转换（PDF 转 Word、Excel、PPT、JPG 及反向）、压缩、合并、拆分、页面旋转、电子签名、解除密码保护，付费套餐还有 OCR。

How To Use It怎么用

  1. Go to smallpdf.com打开 smallpdf.com and pick the tool you need.打开 smallpdf.com，选择你需要的工具。
  2. Drop your PDF into the drop zone.把 PDF 拖进拖拽区。
  3. Adjust any options for that tool (compression level, page range, target format).按需调整该工具的选项（压缩级别、页面范围、目标格式）。
  4. Process the file, then download the result.处理文件，然后下载结果。
  5. Note the daily task counter — free use is limited, and files are usually removed from their servers after a short period.注意每日任务计数——免费使用有限，而且文件通常在短时间后就会从他们的服务器上删除。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

Is Smallpdf free?Smallpdf 免费吗？

Partly. There is a free tier usable without registration, but it limits tasks per day and file size. Unlimited access requires a subscription.部分是。有无需注册即可使用的免费版，但限制每日任务数和文件大小。无限使用需要订阅。

How many free tasks do I get?免费版能做多少次任务？

A limited number per day. The exact allowance changes, so check the current terms on their site.每天有次数限制。具体额度会变动，请查看他们官网的当前说明。

Is there a watermark?有水印吗？

Most tools do not add a watermark, though the free tier restricts how much you can do.多数工具不会加水印，不过免费版会限制你能做多少。

Can it convert PDF to Word?能把 PDF 转成 Word 吗？

Yes, in both directions, and formatting fidelity is generally good for text-based documents.可以，双向都支持，而且对以文字为主的文档，格式保真度通常不错。

Are my files stored?我的文件会被保存吗？

Files are uploaded for processing and removed from their servers after a short retention period. Do not use it for highly confidential documents.文件会上传处理，并在短暂的保留期后从他们的服务器上删除。高度机密的文档请勿使用。

Does it support e-signature?支持电子签名吗？

Yes, you can sign PDFs electronically and request signatures from others.可以，你能对 PDF 进行电子签名，也可以向他人请求签名。
