PicCalc (PicStudio)

Most "free online image tools" quietly upload your photo to a server, process it, and hand it back. PicCalc takes the opposite approach: all 24 of its tools run inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly, so your pictures never leave your device. That single design choice makes it worth a look — especially if you handle ID photos, contracts or client artwork.大多数「免费在线图片工具」都会悄悄把你的照片传到服务器，处理完再返回。PicCalc 走了相反的路：它全部 24 个工具都用 JavaScript 和 WebAssembly 在你的浏览器里运行，图片永远不会离开你的设备。光是这一个设计选择就值得一看——尤其当你经常处理证件照、合同或客户稿件时。

What It Is这是什么

PicCalc (branded PicStudio on the site) is a single-page image workbench that packs 24 separate tools into one interface. Instead of bouncing between five different websites to remove a background, resize the result, and then convert it to PNG, you stay in one tab and move between tools with a click.PicCalc（站内品牌名为 PicStudio）是一个单页图片工作台，把 24 个独立工具塞进了同一个界面。你不用在五个不同网站之间跳来跳去——先抠图、再改尺寸、最后转 PNG——而是留在同一个标签页里，点一下就切换工具。

The technical foundation matters here. Everything — the AI background removal model, the filters, the compression — executes client-side in your browser. When you drop a photo in, it is decoded into a canvas element and manipulated in memory. There is no upload step, no processing queue, and no server-side copy of your file. For sensitive material this removes an entire category of risk.这里的技术基础很关键。所有环节——AI 抠图模型、滤镜、压缩——都在你的浏览器本地执行。当你把照片拖进去，它会被解码成 canvas 元素，然后在内存中处理。没有上传步骤，没有处理队列，服务器上也不存在你文件的副本。对敏感材料来说，这直接消除了一整类风险。

It is also genuinely free, with no registration wall. You land on the page and can immediately start using any tool. There is no "three free exports per day" counter and no watermark stamped onto results — which is where many well-known alternatives start charging.它也是真的免费，没有注册墙。打开页面就能立刻开始用任何工具。没有「每天三次免费导出」的计数器，结果上也不会被打水印——而很多知名替代品恰恰就是从这里开始收费的。

How To Use It怎么用

  1. Open piccalc.com — no sign-up, no installation.打开 piccalc.com——无需注册，无需安装。
  2. Pick a tool from the grid. Each one opens as its own workspace.从工具网格里挑一个。每个工具都会打开自己的工作区。
  3. Drop your image in, or click to select a file. Large photos are handled fine since nothing is uploaded.把图片拖进去，或点击选择文件。因为不会上传，大照片也能处理好。
  4. Adjust the settings for that tool — slider, colour choice, target size.调整该工具的设置——滑块、颜色选择、目标尺寸。
  5. Preview the result live; most tools show the original beside the output.实时预览结果；多数工具会把原图和输出并排显示。
  6. Click download. The finished file is written straight from your browser's memory to your disk.点击下载。成品文件直接从你的浏览器内存写入磁盘。
tip实用提示 可以在同一张图上连续用几个工具 —— 比如先去背景，再切到证件照工具换成蓝底，最后压缩。因为全部在本地完成，串联多步不会产生额外开销。

Privacy Why It Matters隐私为什么重要

This is the part most tool directories skip, and it is PicCalc's real advantage.这一点是多数工具目录站都会略过的，而这正是 PicCalc 真正的优势。

When you use a typical cloud-based background remover, your photo travels over the network to somebody's server, gets stored at least temporarily, and is deleted according to a policy you have probably never read. If that photo is a passport scan, a signed contract, an unreleased product shot or a client's private portrait, you have just handed it to a third party.当你使用一个典型的云端抠图工具时，你的照片会通过网络传到别人的服务器，至少被临时存储，然后按一份你大概从没读过的策略被删除。如果那张照片是护照扫描件、签好的合同、未发布的产品图或客户的私人肖像，你等于刚把它交给了一个第三方。

With browser-side processing, the file physically never leaves your machine. You can verify this yourself: open the browser's network tab while using the tool and watch — there is no large outbound upload. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and most tools keep working.采用浏览器端处理后，文件在物理上永远不会离开你的电脑。你可以自己验证：用这个工具时打开浏览器的网络面板观察——没有任何大体积的出站上传。你甚至可以在页面加载完成后断开网络，多数工具依然能用。

For anyone handling identity documents, medical images, legal paperwork or pre-release creative work, this alone justifies keeping PicCalc bookmarked.对任何要处理身份证件、医疗影像、法律文件或未发布创意稿的人来说，光是这一点就值得把 PicCalc 放进书签。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

Does PicCalc upload my photos to a server?PicCalc 会把我的照片上传到服务器吗？

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your image is decoded into the page and manipulated in your device's memory; no copy is sent anywhere.不会。所有处理都通过 JavaScript 和 WebAssembly 在你的浏览器本地完成。你的图片被解码进页面，并在你设备的内存中处理；不会有任何副本被发送到别处。

Is PicCalc really free, and is there a watermark?PicCalc 真的免费吗？会加水印吗？

Yes — all 24 tools are free with no registration required, and exported images carry no watermark.是的——24 个工具全部免费，无需注册，导出的图片也没有水印。

Do I need to install anything or create an account?需要安装什么或注册账号吗？

Neither. It runs in a normal browser tab on desktop or mobile. No extension, no app, no login.都不是。它在桌面或手机的普通浏览器标签页里运行。无需扩展、无需 App、无需登录。

Can it make ID or visa photos with a red or blue background?能做红底或蓝底的证件照、签证照吗？

Yes. The ID Photo tool builds correctly sized ID images and lets you switch the background colour, including the red and blue backdrops required by various official photo specifications.可以。证件照工具能生成尺寸合规的证件照，并允许你切换背景色，包括各类官方照片规范所要求的红底和蓝底。

Can I use it on my phone?手机上能用吗？

Yes, the layout adapts to mobile browsers. Very large images may process more slowly on a phone than on a desktop.可以，布局会适配移动浏览器。超大图片在手机上可能比在台式机上处理得慢。

How is this different from Remove.bg or TinyPNG?它和 Remove.bg、TinyPNG 有什么不同？

Those are single-purpose cloud services: excellent at their one task, but your file is uploaded, and free tiers limit size or add watermarks. PicCalc keeps the file local, has no quota, and bundles 24 tools in one place.那些都是单一用途的云服务：在自己的那一件事上表现出色，但你的文件会被上传，而且免费版会限制尺寸或加水印。PicCalc 让文件留在本地、没有次数限制，还把 24 个工具集中在一处。
