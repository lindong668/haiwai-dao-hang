CloudConvert

Updated Oct 2026Category: File ConverterHands-on tested

CloudConvert is built for people who care about output quality. Rather than a single convert button, it exposes codec, resolution, bitrate and format options so you control exactly what comes out.

What It Is

Most converters hide their settings. CloudConvert does the opposite — you can choose the video codec, target resolution, audio bitrate, image quality and format-specific parameters before running a job. That makes it the right choice when the defaults of a simpler tool are not good enough.

Coverage spans documents, spreadsheets, presentations, images, audio, video, ebooks, archives and CAD/CAM files. Cloud imports from Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and S3, and an API supports automated pipelines.

The free tier works on a daily allowance of conversion minutes rather than a file count, which suits occasional larger jobs less well than many small ones.

How To Use It

  1. Go to cloudconvert.com
  2. Select the source and target formats.
  3. Upload your file or import from cloud storage.
  4. Open the options panel and set codec, resolution, bitrate or quality as needed.
  5. Convert and download. Watch your daily conversion-minute allowance on the free tier.

Strengths And Limitations

What works well

  • Granular control over output quality and format parameters
  • Very broad format coverage including video and audio
  • Imports from major cloud storage providers
  • API for automated conversion pipelines
  • Reliable output quality

Limitations

  • Free tier measured in conversion minutes, so large jobs consume it fast
  • Options panel can intimidate casual users
  • Files are uploaded to their servers
  • Heavy users will need a paid plan

Who Should Use It

Video and audio editors needing controlled transcodes, developers automating conversion through the API, and anyone whose output quality requirements exceed what a one-click converter provides.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CloudConvert free?

There is a free tier with a daily allowance of conversion minutes and no registration for small jobs. Volume requires a paid plan.

What is a conversion minute?

The free allowance is measured by processing time rather than file count, so long video files use it up faster than small documents.

Can I control output quality?

Yes — codec, resolution, bitrate, quality and other format-specific settings are available before conversion.

Does it handle video and audio?

Yes, extensively, which is where its detailed options matter most.

Is there an API?

Yes, suited to automated and bulk conversion workflows.

Are files uploaded?

Yes, processing is server-side. Avoid it for confidential material.
