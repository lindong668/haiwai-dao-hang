What It Is这是什么

The tool is aimed at writers who need to express the same idea differently — avoiding accidental repetition, smoothing awkward phrasing, or making a draft read more naturally. Multiple modes let you pick the intensity and character of the rewrite rather than getting one fixed transformation.这工具面向的是需要用不同方式表达同一个意思的写作者——避免无意的重复、理顺别扭的措辞，或让草稿读起来更自然。多种模式让你可以选择改写的强度和风格，而不是只能得到一种固定的结果。

It also bundles supporting utilities: a grammar checker, a summariser, a plagiarism checker and a word counter, which makes it a reasonable single stop for draft cleanup.它还打包了一些辅助工具：语法检查、摘要生成、查重和字数统计，因此算是一个还算合适的「草稿清理一站式」选择。

The free tier limits words per submission; registering raises it.免费版限制单次提交的字数；注册后额度会提高。

How To Use It怎么用

  1. Go to paraphraser.io.
  2. Paste your text into the box (free submissions are word-limited).把文本粘进输入框（免费提交有字数限制）。
  3. Choose a mode — fluency, standard, creative, smarter or similar, depending on how heavy a rewrite you want.选择模式——fluency、standard、creative、smarter 等，取决于你想要多大幅度的改写。
  4. Click paraphrase and read the output carefully.点击改写，然后仔细阅读输出结果。
  5. Edit the result. Treat it as a draft to be reviewed, not a finished passage.对结果进行编辑。把它当成需要审校的草稿，而不是成稿。
tip实用提示 用能达到目的的最轻模式。激进改写常常带来细微的意思偏移或别扭措辞，所以保留自己的句式、只做轻度润色，通常比整体重构读起来更好。
