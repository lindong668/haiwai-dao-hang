Adobe Firefly

Updated Oct 20262026年10月更新Category: AI Online Tools分类：AI 在线工具Hands-on tested实测收录

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's generative AI suite: text-to-image generation, generative fill for adding or removing objects in existing photos, text effects, and vector recolouring. Its distinguishing feature is training on licensed and public-domain content.Adobe Firefly 是 Adobe 的生成式 AI 套件：文生图、可在现有照片中增删物体的生成式填充、文字特效，以及矢量重新配色。它最大的特点是训练数据来自授权内容与公有领域素材。

What It Is这是什么

Firefly's main claim is provenance. Adobe trained it on Adobe Stock licensed material and public-domain content rather than scraping the open web, and generated images carry Content Credentials — tamper-evident metadata recording how the image was made. For commercial users worried about copyright exposure, that is a meaningful difference from many competitors.Firefly 主打的是「来源可溯」。Adobe 用的是 Adobe Stock 授权素材和公有领域内容，而不是抓取公开网络；生成的图片还带 Content Credentials——一种防篡改元数据，记录这张图是怎么做出来的。对担心版权风险的商业用户来说，这是它区别于众多竞品的实质差异。

The features go beyond simple generation. Generative Fill lets you select part of a photograph and describe what should be there (or select something and make it vanish). Text Effects renders stylised typography. Generative Recolor produces colour variants of vector artwork.它的功能不止于简单生成。生成式填充让你可以选中照片的某个区域，描述那里该有什么（或者选中某个物体让它消失）。文字特效可渲染风格化字体。生成式重新配色能为矢量图稿产出多种配色方案。

Access is tied to an Adobe account, and generation consumes monthly generative credits. Light use is possible on the free tier; regular work generally needs a paid plan.使用需要 Adobe 账号，生成会消耗每月的生成额度。免费版可以轻度使用；要常态化工作一般得买付费套餐。

How To Use It怎么用

  1. Go to firefly.adobe.com打开 firefly.adobe.com and sign in with an Adobe account.打开 firefly.adobe.com，用 Adobe 账号登录。
  2. Choose a mode: text to image, generative fill, text effects or recolor.选择模式：文生图、生成式填充、文字特效或重新配色。
  3. Describe what you want. Add style, lighting and composition details for better control.描述你想要的内容。补充风格、光线和构图细节，控制效果会更好。
  4. Pick from the generated variations and refine the prompt, or adjust settings like aspect ratio.从生成的多个版本里挑选，并优化提示词，或调整宽高比等设置。
  5. Download the result. Commercial use terms depend on your plan and Adobe's current licensing terms.下载结果。商业使用条款取决于你的套餐以及 Adobe 当前的授权条款。

Strengths And Limitations优点与不足

What works well好用的地方

  • Trained on licensed and public-domain content, reducing copyright risk基于授权与公有领域内容训练，降低版权风险
  • Content Credentials record how an image was createdContent Credentials 记录图像的生成方式
  • Generative Fill for adding or removing objects in existing photos生成式填充可在现有照片中增删物体
  • Integrates with the wider Adobe ecosystem可与整个 Adobe 生态打通
  • Text effects and vector recolor are genuinely useful for designers文字特效与矢量改色对设计师确实实用

Limitations不足之处

  • Requires an Adobe account and consumes monthly credits需要 Adobe 账号，且消耗每月额度
  • Free allowance is limited for anyone generating regularly对经常生成的人来说，免费额度偏少
  • Output style is somewhat conservative compared with more experimental models相比更激进的模型，输出风格偏保守
  • Content Credentials metadata may need removing for certain workflows某些工作流下可能需要移除 Content Credentials 元数据

Who Should Use It适合谁用

Designers, marketers and small businesses who need commercially defensible AI imagery, and Adobe subscribers who want generation inside a familiar ecosystem. Casual users can try the free credits, but heavy use means a subscription.需要「商业上站得住脚」的 AI 图像的设计师、市场人员和小企业，以及希望在熟悉生态里完成生成的 Adobe 订阅用户。普通用户可以用免费额度试试，但重度使用就得订阅了。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

Is Adobe Firefly free?Adobe Firefly 免费吗？

There is a free tier with a monthly allowance of generative credits, enough for occasional use. Regular generation requires a paid Creative Cloud plan.有免费版，每月给一定生成额度，偶尔用用够。经常生成需要付费的 Creative Cloud 套餐。

Do I need an Adobe account?需要 Adobe 账号吗？

Yes, sign-in is required even for the free tier.是的，即使是免费版也需要登录。

Can I use generated images commercially?生成的图片可以商用吗？

Adobe positions Firefly as commercially safer because it was trained on licensed and public-domain content, but you should review the current licence terms for your specific plan before commercial use.Adobe 把 Firefly 定位为「商业上更安全」，因为它基于授权和公有领域内容训练；但商用前你仍应针对自己的套餐查看当前授权条款。

What are Content Credentials?什么是 Content Credentials？

Tamper-evident metadata attached to the file recording how it was generated. It is designed to make AI provenance traceable.附加在文件上的防篡改元数据，记录图像是如何生成的。它的设计目的是让 AI 来源可追溯。

What is generative fill?什么是生成式填充？

A feature that lets you select part of an existing photo and describe what should appear there, or select an object and have it removed with the background plausibly rebuilt.一项功能：选中现有照片的某个区域，描述那里该出现什么；或选中某个物体让它被移除，同时把背景合理地重建出来。

Does it beat other AI image generators?它比其他 AI 绘图工具强吗？

It is not always the most stylistically adventurous, but its licensing position and Adobe integration make it the safer choice for commercial design work.它在风格上未必最大胆，但它的授权立场和 Adobe 生态集成，使其成为商业设计工作中更稳妥的选择。
