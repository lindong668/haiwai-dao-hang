What It Is这是什么

The underlying AI is trained specifically on foreground/background separation, which is why it handles the cases that defeat generic selection tools — flyaway hair, fur, semi-transparent fabric, bicycle spokes and complex outlines. It also detects shadows and can optionally keep or drop them.它底层的 AI 是专门针对前景/背景分离训练的，所以能搞定那些让通用选择工具束手无策的情况——飞散的头发、毛发、半透明织物、自行车辐条和复杂轮廓。它还能识别阴影，并可选择保留或去掉。

You can use it without an account. The important caveat is resolution: the free download is a small preview image, typically around 500–600 pixels on the long edge. That is fine for a mockup, a presentation slide or a quick test, but far too small for print or for a product listing where buyers zoom in. Full resolution and HD output require credits or a paid plan.不用账号也能用。但有个重要提醒：分辨率。免费下载的是一张小预览图，长边通常只有 500–600 像素左右。做样机图、PPT 幻灯片或快速测试够用，但要印刷或用在买家会放大的商品详情页就远远不够。全分辨率和 HD 输出需要积分或付费套餐。

There are also integrations and an API for bulk processing, which is where it becomes genuinely useful for e-commerce sellers handling hundreds of product photos.它还提供集成和用于批量处理的 API，这对要处理数百张商品照的电商卖家来说才是真正有用之处。

How To Use It怎么用

  1. Go to remove.bg — no sign-up needed for the basic tool.打开 remove.bg——基础工具无需注册。
  2. Upload an image or paste a URL. Processing usually finishes in about five seconds.上传图片或粘贴网址。处理通常约五秒完成。
  3. Preview the result. Use Edit → Erase/Restore to fix any areas the AI missed.预览结果。用 Edit → Erase/Restore 修补 AI 漏掉的区域。
  4. Add a new background: solid colour, a custom image, or drop shadow options.添加新背景：纯色、自定义图片，或投影选项。
  5. Download. Note that the free version exports at reduced resolution; full size needs credits.下载。注意免费版导出的是降分辨率版本；原尺寸需要积分。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

How large are free downloads?免费下载的尺寸有多大？
