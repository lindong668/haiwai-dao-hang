What It Is这是什么

Most online converters specialise: one site does images, another does video, a third does documents. Convertio covers essentially all of them in one interface, which saves you from hunting for a new website every time an unusual format appears.多数在线转换器都很专一：这个站只做图片，那个只做视频，另一个只做文档。Convertio 在一个界面里几乎全包了，省得你每次遇到冷门格式都要重新找网站。

Beyond straight conversion it includes OCR (optical character recognition) for extracting text from images and scanned PDFs, basic video options like resolution and codec settings, and archive handling so you can pull files out of a zip without installing software.除了直接转换，它还有 OCR（光学字符识别）可从图片和扫描 PDF 中提取文字，有分辨率、编码器等基础视频选项，还能处理压缩包——不用装软件就能从 zip 里把文件取出来。

Files are uploaded for processing, and the free tier caps file size. Registration raises the limit substantially.文件需要上传处理，免费版有文件大小上限。注册后上限会大幅提高。

How To Use It怎么用

  1. Go to convertio.co — the converter loads without an account.打开 convertio.co，没有账号也能直接用。
  2. Choose the target format, or let it suggest one after you upload.选择目标格式，或上传后让它推荐一个。
  3. Upload from your computer, Google Drive, Dropbox or a URL.从电脑、Google Drive、Dropbox 或网址上传。
  4. Adjust any format-specific options (resolution, codec, quality, OCR language).按需调整格式专属选项（分辨率、编码器、质量、OCR 语言）。
  5. Convert, then download. Larger files require signing in or a paid plan.转换后下载。较大的文件需要登录或付费套餐。
tip实用提示 转换 PDF 时，如果源文件是扫描件而不是文字版 PDF，要明确勾选 OCR。不做 OCR，你会得到一份看起来正常、但里面没有可选中文字的文档。

Strengths And Limitations优点与不足

What works well好用的地方

  • Enormous format coverage — one tool instead of five格式覆盖极广——一个顶五个
  • OCR built in for scanned documents and images内置 OCR，可处理扫描文档与图片
  • Imports directly from cloud storage or a URL可直接从云存储或网址导入
  • Video options for codec, resolution and quality提供编码器、分辨率与质量的视频选项
  • Handles archives and ebooks, which many converters ignore支持压缩包和电子书，很多转换器都不管这两种

Limitations不足之处

  • Free file-size cap is restrictive for video and large PDFs免费版的文件大小上限对视频和大 PDF 来说偏紧

  • Conversion speed depends on queue and file size转换速度取决于排队情况和文件大小
  • Some advanced options sit behind a paid plan部分高级选项需要付费套餐

Who Should Use It适合谁用

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

Is Convertio free?Convertio 免费吗？

There is a free tier with a file-size cap and no registration for small files. Bigger files and heavier use require an account or paid subscription.有免费版，小文件无需注册但有大小上限。更大的文件和更高频的使用需要账号或付费订阅。

What is the maximum free file size?免费版最大支持多大的文件？

The free tier is capped at a size suitable for small images and short audio; video and large PDFs generally need an account. Check the current limit on their site, as it changes.免费版的上限适合小图片和短音频；视频和大 PDF 一般都需要账号。具体上限会变动，请以他们官网当前说明为准。

Does it do OCR?它支持 OCR 吗？

Yes — it can extract text from images and scanned PDFs, and you can select the document language for better accuracy.可以——它能从图片和扫描 PDF 中提取文字，还能选择文档语言以提高准确率。

Can I convert video?能转换视频吗？

Yes, with options for format, codec, resolution and quality, though free conversions are limited by file size and duration.可以，支持格式、编码器、分辨率和质量选项，不过免费转换受文件大小和时长限制。

Are my files uploaded?我的文件会被上传吗？

Yes, processing is server-side. Avoid using it for confidential documents.是的，处理在服务器端完成，机密文档请勿使用。

Can I pull files from Google Drive?能直接从 Google Drive 取文件吗？

Yes, it imports from Google Drive, Dropbox and direct URLs.可以，它支持从 Google Drive、Dropbox 和直接网址导入。
