What It Is这是什么

PriceScope is a real-time price site built on Cloudflare Workers. It pulls commodity and currency data and renders the actual numbers into the HTML before the page reaches your browser. That detail sounds technical but has two practical consequences: the page loads with figures already visible (no blank boxes while scripts run), and search engines can read the real values — which is why its pages get indexed cleanly.PriceScope 是一个构建在 Cloudflare Workers 上的实时价格站。它抓取大宗商品和汇率数据，并在页面送达你浏览器之前，就把真实数字渲染进 HTML 里。这个细节听着技术，却带来两个实际好处：页面一打开数字就已经在那儿（不会先空着等脚本跑完），而且搜索引擎能读到真实数值 —— 这也是它的页面能被干净收录的原因。

The site covers four distinct data areas, each with its own dedicated page:站点覆盖四个独立的数据板块，各有专属页面：

  • International Crude Oil — Brent and WTI benchmarks, the two reference prices nearly every oil article quotes国际原油——布伦特与 WTI 两大基准，几乎所有石油文章都会引用的两个参考价
  • Gold — quoted both per troy ounce in USD and per gram in CNY, so you can check either the global benchmark or the price your local jeweller is referencing黄金——同时以美元/金衡盎司和元/克两种单位报价，你既能查国际基准价，也能查本地金店所参考的价格
  • China Retail Fuel — 92, 95, 98 and 0# diesel prices for all 31 provinces and regions国内成品油——全国 31 个省市自治区的 92、95、98 号汽油及 0# 柴油价格
  • Currency — a converter plus the most-requested exchange rate pairs货币——一个换算器，外加最常被查询的汇率货币对

It is offered in seven languages — English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. The language is part of the URL (for example /en/oil/ or /zh/gold/), so you can bookmark or share a specific language version directly.它提供七种语言 —— 英语、中文、日语、西班牙语、法语、德语和葡萄牙语。语言就写在网址里（比如 /en/oil/ 或 /zh/gold/），所以你可以直接把某个语言版本加书签或分享出去。

What It Tracks它追踪哪些数据

Crude oil — Brent and WTI原油 —— 布伦特与 WTI

These are the two benchmarks that matter. Brent is the international reference, sourced from North Sea production, and it is what most of the world's oil is priced against. WTI (West Texas Intermediate) is the US benchmark, delivered at Cushing, Oklahoma, and typically trades a few dollars below Brent. The gap between them — the "Brent-WTI spread" — is itself closely watched as a signal about US supply, shipping capacity and global demand.这是两个真正重要的基准。布伦特是国际参考价，产自北海，全球大部分石油都以它为定价基准。WTI（西德克萨斯中质原油）是美国基准，在俄克拉荷马州库欣交割，通常比布伦特低几美元。两者之间的价差——「布伦特-WTI 价差」——本身就被密切关注，被视为反映美国供应、运输能力和全球需求的信号。

PriceScope shows both, so you can see the spread rather than having to look up two sites and subtract.PriceScope 两个都显示，所以你能直接看到价差，而不必查两个网站再做减法。

Gold — per ounce and per gram黄金 —— 按盎司与按克

Gold is quoted globally in US dollars per troy ounce, but retail buyers in China think in yuan per gram. Converting between them is a small but constant annoyance: one troy ounce is about 31.1035 grams, and then you need the current USD/CNY rate on top. PriceScope displays both units, removing the manual arithmetic.黄金在国际上按美元/金衡盎司报价，但国内零售买家习惯按元/克思考。两者换算是个不大却一直存在的麻烦：一金衡盎司约等于 31.1035 克，之后还得再乘上当下的美元兑人民币汇率。PriceScope 同时显示两种单位，省掉你手动计算。

China fuel — all 31 provinces国内油价 —— 覆盖全部 31 个省份

This is the feature with the least competition. China adjusts retail fuel prices on a published cycle, and the result differs by province — Beijing's 95-octane is not priced identically to Guangdong's. PriceScope lists 92, 95 and 98 gasoline plus 0# diesel across all 31 provincial-level regions, which is the number most drivers actually want when the adjustment window comes around.这是竞争最少的一项功能。国内成品油按公布周期调整零售价，且各省结果不同——北京的 95 号汽油和广东的并不一样。PriceScope 列出全部 31 个省级行政区的 92、95、98 号汽油及 0# 柴油价格，而这正是调价窗口来临时多数车主真正想看的数字。

Currency汇率

A straightforward converter plus a set of popular pairs, so you can check a rate without opening a search engine.一个简洁的换算器加上一组常用货币对，让你不用打开搜索引擎就能查汇率。

Why Server Rendering Matters服务端渲染为什么重要

Most price sites are single-page apps: the HTML arrives nearly empty and JavaScript fetches the numbers afterwards. That works for humans but creates two problems — a visible delay before anything appears, and search engines that may index an empty page.多数价格站都是单页应用：HTML 到达时几乎是空的，数字要等 JavaScript 之后再去抓取。对人类用户能用，但带来两个问题——内容出现前有可见延迟，以及搜索引擎可能收录到一个空页面。

PriceScope renders on Cloudflare's edge network before the response is sent. You see numbers on first paint, Google sees the same numbers when it crawls, and the content is delivered from a server close to you rather than from one origin halfway around the world. The practical result is a page that feels instant and that indexes reliably.PriceScope 在响应发送之前就在 Cloudflare 的边缘网络上完成渲染。你在首屏就能看到数字，Google 抓取时看到的是同样的数字，而且内容是从离你近的服务器投递的，而不是来自地球另一端的单一源站。实际结果就是一个感觉极快、且能被可靠收录的页面。

What is the difference between Brent and WTI?布伦特和 WTI 有什么区别？
