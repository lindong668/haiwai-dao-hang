ChatPDF turns a PDF into something you can interrogate. Upload a paper, contract or manual, then ask questions in plain language and get answers that cite the specific pages they came from.ChatPDF 把 PDF 变成一个可以「提问」的对象。上传论文、合同或说明书，然后用大白话提问，得到的答案会注明出自哪一页。

What It Is这是什么

The value is speed of comprehension. A 60-page report becomes a conversation: ask for the summary, ask what the methodology was, ask whether a particular number appears anywhere, and get an answer with a page reference you can jump to and verify.它的价值在于理解速度。一份 60 页的报告变成一场对话：要摘要、问研究方法是什么、问某个数字有没有出现过，答案都会带上页码，你可以直接跳过去核对。

The citations are what make it trustworthy rather than merely impressive. Each answer links back to the passage it was derived from, so you can check the source instead of taking the model's word — essential for academic and legal material.正是这些引用让它值得信赖，而不只是「看着厉害」。每条答案都链回它所依据的原文段落，你可以自己去核对，而不是盲目相信模型的说法——这对学术和法律材料尤为关键。

It works on research papers, manuals, financial reports, legal documents and books. The free tier limits how many documents and questions you can use per day.它适用于研究论文、说明书、财报、法律文书和书籍。免费版会限制每天可处理的文档数和提问次数。

How To Use It怎么用

  1. Go to chatpdf.com打开 chatpdf.com — no registration needed to try it.打开 chatpdf.com，试用无需注册。
  2. Drag in a PDF (or paste a link to one).把 PDF 拖进去（或粘贴一个 PDF 链接）。
  3. Wait for it to be parsed, usually under a minute for typical documents.等待解析，一般文档通常不到一分钟。
  4. Ask questions in plain language; suggested starter questions are offered.用大白话提问；它也会给出一些建议的起始问题。
  5. Click the page citation on any answer to jump to the source passage and verify.点击答案上的页码引用，就能跳到原文段落进行核对。
tip实用提示 先要摘要，再追问细节。一上来就问得很窄，往往只能得到浅层回答；先把整篇文档的结构建立起来，模型后续回答更细的问题时才有上下文。

Strengths And Limitations优点与不足

What works well好用的地方

  • Citations let you verify every answer against the source引用让每条答案都可回溯原文核对
  • Dramatically faster than reading long documents cover to cover比通读长文档快得多
  • No registration to try, with suggested starter questions无需注册即可试用，还提供起始问题建议
  • Handles dense academic and legal material well对密集的学术与法律材料处理得不错
  • Plain-language questions — no query syntax to learn用自然语言提问，不用学查询语法

Limitations不足之处

  • Free daily limits on documents and questions免费版每天限制文档数与提问数
  • Documents are uploaded — unsuitable for confidential files文档会上传——不适合机密文件
  • Heavily scanned PDFs without a text layer may parse poorly纯扫描件若没有文字层，解析效果可能很差
  • Answers can occasionally be overconfident; always check the citation答案偶尔会过于自信，务必核对引用

Who Should Use It适合谁用

Students working through research papers, professionals digesting long reports, and anyone facing a contract or manual who wants the key points without reading every page. Avoid it for confidential documents.啃研究论文的学生、需要消化长篇报告的职场人，以及面对合同或说明书只想抓重点、不想逐页读完的人。机密文档请勿使用。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

Is ChatPDF free?ChatPDF 免费吗？

Yes for basic use, with daily limits on the number of documents and questions. Paid plans raise those limits.基础使用免费，但每天限制文档数与提问次数。付费套餐会提高额度。

Do I need to sign up?需要注册吗？

No, you can try it without an account. Signing in preserves your chat history and raises limits.不用，没有账号也能试用。登录可以保存聊天记录并提高额度。

Are the answers reliable?它的回答可靠吗？

Answers come with citations to the source pages, and you should click through to verify — especially for legal, medical or financial content.答案会附带原文页码引用，你应该点进去核对——法律、医疗或金融类内容尤其如此。

Does it work on scanned PDFs?扫描版 PDF 能用吗？

Poorly if the file has no text layer. OCR the scan first for usable results.如果文件没有文字层，效果会很差。请先对扫描件做 OCR 识别再上传。

Is my document uploaded?我的文档会被上传吗？

Yes, processing is server-side, so do not use it for confidential material.是的，处理在服务器端完成，因此不要用于机密材料。

How large a PDF can it handle?它能处理多大的 PDF？

Typical documents parse fine; very large files may be limited on the free tier.常规文档解析没问题；特别大的文件在免费版上可能会受限。
